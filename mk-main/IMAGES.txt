MK PHOTO GUIDE
==============
Put your own photos in these folders. Anything missing automatically falls back to the built-in drawing,
so you can add them a few at a time. Use photos you own or have a licence for (your own shoot, a supplier's
press kit, or stock like Unsplash/Pexels where the licence allows commercial use). Do not copy photos from other brands' sites.

PRODUCT PHOTOS  ->  shop/public/img/p/
  * Format: transparent PNG cut-out of the product on its own, about 1000 x 1250 px, product centred, a little space around it.
  * Main photo:            pNN.png        (NN = the number below, e.g. p01.png)
  * One photo per colour:  pNN-S.png      (S = colour number as shown on the page, starting at 1, e.g. p01-3.png = 3rd shade)
    If a colour photo is missing, the main photo is used.
  * Sizes (ml): only ONE photo is needed. The site scales it up/down when someone picks 30 ml / 50 ml / 100 ml.
  * Makeup (lips, eyes, face): the model try-on stays; your product photo appears as a small thumbnail in the corner.

MODEL PHOTOS  ->  shop/public/img/m/
  * m1.jpg = home page hero, m2.jpg = About section, m3.jpg = Shade Finder. 4:5 portrait JPG, about 1200 x 1500 px.
  * Show a range of skin tones across them (light, medium, deep).
  * m4.jpg = Бидний түүх (Our Story) portrait — Unsplash photo 1531746020798-e6953c6e8e04 (real
    photography, Unsplash licence). Tried FIRST; CDN URL is the fallback, m2.jpg the last resort.
  * m5.jpg = "Шинэ" ad card 1 (glossy-lips beauty portrait) — Unsplash 1529626455594-4ff0802cfb7e.
  * m6.jpg = "Шинэ" ad card 2 (glowing skin portrait) — Unsplash 1519699047748-de8e457a634e.
  * m7.jpg = "Шинэ" ad card 3 (makeup application close-up) — Unsplash 1515688594390-b649af70d282.
